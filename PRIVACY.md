1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how Allagosh Systems® ("we", "us") handles information in the official AXBet application. AXBet is an official cricket feed and match center app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data We Collect
AXBet v15.0 is designed to work with minimal data collection.

a) Data NOT Collected:

We do NOT collect real name, phone number, or ID documents
We do NOT collect banking information or real-money betting history
We do NOT collect precise location (GPS)
b) Data Collected Automatically:

Anonymous app usage (e.g., which screens are viewed) for crash fixing
Device model, Android version, and app version to fix bugs
Firebase Crashlytics logs if the app crashes
3. How We Use Data
To maintain and improve app performance and stability
To fix bugs and prevent crashes
To ensure the official com.axbet.app build is working correctly
4. Third-Party Services
AXBet v15.0 uses only official Google services:

Firebase / Firestore - For app config and crash reports
Google Play Services - For basic device checks
We do NOT share data with betting companies or ad networks in this official build. No data is sold.

5. Data Storage
All logs are stored securely on Firebase servers. Crash logs are automatically deleted after 90 days.

6. Children's Privacy
AXBet is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights
You can:

Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection
Request deletion of crash logs by emailing us at fajr.ent@gmail.com with your device ID
